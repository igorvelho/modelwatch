What the first version does

No customer integration. No payment. No promise of perfect coverage.

For a developer

  • Follow providers or models.
  • Read the exact change and its source.
  • Export a JSON event or subscribe to a digest.
  • Use the warning to open a migration task.

For the beta

  • Three providers first.
  • Daily snapshots, not real-time monitoring.
  • Public changelog before a full API.
  • Free signal collection before charging.
